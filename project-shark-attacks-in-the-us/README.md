# Shark Attacks in the US

## Project Overview

Analyzes shark attack data to provide insights into the frequency and circumstances of attacks in the USA.

---

## Business Problem

Shark attacks pose a significant risk to beachgoers in the USA, with an average of 16 attacks occurring each year. Understanding the patterns and causes of these attacks can help mitigate risks and improve safety for swimmers and surfers.

---

## Objective

- Analyze shark attack data from 1970 to 2019 to identify trends.
- Develop insights into the relationship between human activities and shark behavior.
- Provide recommendations for reducing the risk of shark attacks.

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Excel
- DAX
- Power Query
- Data Visualization

---

## Project Workflow

- Collect and clean shark attack data from various sources.
- Load the cleaned data into Power BI for analysis.
- Create measures and calculated columns to analyze trends over decades.
- Visualize the data to identify patterns and provide insights.
- Develop recommendations based on the findings to enhance safety.

---

## Key Insights

- There are an average of 16 shark attacks per year in the United States, with one fatality every two years.
- Only three shark species (Great White, Tiger, and Bull Sharks) are responsible for a significant number of attacks.
- Shark attacks have occurred in almost every coastal state, with Florida, Hawaii, and California being the most affected.
